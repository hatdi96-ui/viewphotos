The Transparency Loop

How We Guarantee Proof of Your Impact

No mystery. No vague reports. See exactly where your contribution goes in three simple steps.

1

You Back a Classroom

Choose a verified teacher project or contribute to our general classroom supply fund through partners like DonorsChoose.

2

Direct Supply Delivery

100% of your gift is turned into physical goods—laptops, art kits, books—and shipped straight to the teacher's classroom door.

3

Visual Proof Sent to You

Teachers take photos of students actively using the funded materials and write a personalized thank-you update delivered right to your inbox.
